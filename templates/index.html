#All @ your Fingertips

-~ How to Use ~-

  1. Detection: Show your palm straight 🖐 to the camera.
  2. Selection: Hover over your choice with your index + middle fingers up ✌.
  3. Painting: Just use your index ☝ finger to sketch your thoughts on air!

-~ Quick Demo ~-

Recommendations for better results:

  • background clarity: avoid noisy bg
  • hand movements: try avoiding discrete and swift movements
  • distance from the camera: preferably not too far
